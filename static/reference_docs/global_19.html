Chapter 1: Dystocia Management & Obstetrical Maneuvers

Correction Hierarchy in Livestock Dystocia:

1. Mutation: Repulsion (pushing fetus back into uterus), Rotation (turning along longitudinal axis), and Version (correcting transverse presentation to longitudinal).

2. Traction Rules: Maximum two adults pulling on obstetrical chains; never use motorized mechanical pulling.

3. Cesarean Section vs. Fetotomy: Fetotomy is reserved for dead fetuses when birth canal is patent. If fetus is live or birth canal constricted, execute immediate Cesarean section.
