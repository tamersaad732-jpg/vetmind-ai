Chapter 1: ACVIM Consensus Guidelines for Myxomatous Mitral Valve Disease (MMVD)

Staging & Therapeutic Strategy for Canine MMVD:

  • Stage A: High-risk breeds (Cavalier King Charles Spaniel, Dachshunds) without murmur.
  • Stage B1: Asymptomatic murmur without cardiomegaly (VHS < 10.5, LA:Ao ratio < 1.6). No medical therapy indicated.
  • Stage B2: Asymptomatic murmur WITH cardiac remodeling (VHS > 10.5, LA:Ao > 1.6). Initiate Pimobendan at 0.25-0.3 mg/kg PO q12h (prolongs time to heart failure by ~15 months).
  • Stage C: Acute or chronic congestive heart failure (pulmonary edema). Quad-therapy: Furosemide (2 mg/kg IV/PO q12h), Pimobendan (0.25-0.3 mg/kg q12h), ACE Inhibitor (Enalapril or Benazepril 0.5 mg/kg q12-24h), and Spironolactone (2 mg/kg PO q24h).
  • Stage D: Refractory end-stage heart failure. Add Torsemide (switch from Furosemide at 1/10th dose) and Sildenafil (1-2 mg/kg q8h) if secondary pulmonary hypertension is present.
