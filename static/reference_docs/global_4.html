Chapter 1: Core vs. Non-Core Vaccination Schedules

WSAVA Canine Core Vaccination Protocol:

1. Core Antigens (Essential for all dogs globally):

  • Canine Distemper Virus (CDV), Canine Adenovirus (CAV-1 & CAV-2), Canine Parvovirus type 2 (CPV-2), and Rabies Virus where endemic or legally mandated.

    • 2. Puppy Series: Initial dose at 6-8 weeks, repeat every 2-4 weeks until 16 weeks of age or older (to overcome maternal antibody interference).

    3. Booster Intervals: Complete a booster at 12 months or 26 weeks of age. Subsequent revaccinations for core modified-live virus (MLV) vaccines are recommended at 3-year intervals (triennial), NOT annually.

    WSAVA Feline Core Vaccination Protocol:

    1. Core Antigens: Feline Parvovirus (FPV / Panleukopenia), Feline Herpesvirus-1 (FHV-1), Feline Calicivirus (FCV), and Rabies.

    2. Kitten Series: Begin at 6-8 weeks, repeat q3-4w until 16 weeks or older. Booster at 1 year, then q3y for low-risk indoor cats.

    Chapter 2: Multimodal Pain Management & Glasgow Scoring

    The Glasgow Short-Form Composite Measure Pain Scale (CMPS-SF):

  • Regular bedside assessment of vocalization, posture, mobility, and palpation tenderness.
  • Intervention threshold: Any dog scoring 6/24 (or 5/20 for non-ambulatory) requires immediate rescue analgesia.
  • Multimodal Analgesic Strategy: Pre-emptive administration of an opioid (Methadone, Buprenorphine, or Butorphanol) + NSAID (Meloxicam or Carprofen) + local regional nerve block (Bupivacaine 0.5% or Lidocaine 2%).
