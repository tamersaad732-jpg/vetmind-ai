Chapter 1: Notification of Diseases, Infections and Epizootic Emergencies

Mandatory Criteria for Listing an Animal Disease under WOAH Standards:

1. International spread has been demonstrated or is likely.

2. At least one country has demonstrated freedom or has established an official control zone.

3. The disease has significant zoonotic capacity causing severe disease in humans, or has significant potential for transmission to naive livestock populations causing extreme economic loss.

4. An official validated diagnostic test exists with reliable sensitivity and specificity (OIE Manual of Diagnostic Tests).

Chapter 2: Biosecurity & International Live Animal Quarantine Standards

Standard Protocols for Pre-Export and Port-of-Entry Quarantine:

  • Isolation facility must be an officially approved vector-protected quarantine station.
  • Minimum quarantine holding interval: 21 to 30 continuous days prior to international embarkation.
  • Laboratory screening: Negative serological testing for Brucellosis, Tuberculosis (CFT/PPD intradermal tuberculin test), Foot and Mouth Disease (3ABC non-structural protein ELISA), and Bovine Viral Diarrhea (antigen ELISA or PCR).
