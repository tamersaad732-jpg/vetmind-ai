Chapter 1: Acute Gastric Dilatation-Volvulus (GDV) Emergency Protocol

Emergency Action Plan for GDV in Deep-Chested Dogs:

1. Clinical Presentation: Non-productive retching, restlessness, abdominal tympany, tachycardia with weak femoral pulses (hypovolemic/obstructive shock).

2. Immediate Stabilization:

  • Place two large-bore IV catheters (16-18 gauge) in cephalic or jugular veins (avoid saphenous veins due to caudal vena cava compression).
  • Administer shock fluid resuscitation: LRS 90 mL/kg (give 1/4 dose rapidly over 15 minutes).
  • Gastric decompression: Attempt gentle orogastric intubation with lubricated tube; if unsuccessful, perform immediate percutaneous trocharization using a 14-gauge catheter at the area of maximum tympany.
  • Analgesia: Pure mu-opioid (Hydromorphone 0.1 mg/kg IV or Methadone 0.2 mg/kg IV).
  • Treat ventricular arrhythmias (VPC, ventricular tachycardia): Lidocaine 2 mg/kg IV slow bolus, followed by CRI at 40-80 mcg/kg/min.
